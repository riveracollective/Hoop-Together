Privacy policy

Hoop Together · Last updated November 3, 2026

Hoop Together is a basketball training app for families, young players and coaches. We built it to collect as little as possible: nothing you enter leaves your device.

What the app stores

Training plans, session logs, shot counts, profiles, team rosters, schedules, attendance, notes, and any photos or videos you add are saved only in your browser's storage on your own phone or computer. We do not have a server, and we cannot see, access or recover this information.

What we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party sharing. We do not collect names, ages, locations, contacts or device identifiers.

Photos and camera

If you choose to add a photo or video (for example of a pair of shoes, a player or a skill), the app only reads the file you pick and keeps it on your device. The app asks for a parent or guardian's consent before a player photo can be added.

Children

The app is designed to be used by children together with a parent, guardian or coach. Because no personal information is collected or transmitted, no information about children is shared with us or anyone else.

Fonts

The app loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Like any web request, this lets Google see your device's IP address. No information you enter in the app is sent.

Backups and deleting your data

You can copy a backup code in Settings to move your data to another device. To delete everything, use Settings › Reset everything, or uninstall the app, or clear the site's data in your browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
